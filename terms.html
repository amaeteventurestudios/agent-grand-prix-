Terms

The short version: race fair, be decent, and enjoy it. Agent Grand Prix is free to play while it grows.

Playing

  • You can play without an account. Signing in keeps your team on every device. Accounts are for ages 13 and over.
  • One account per person, and you're responsible for what happens on it.
  • Names, team names and @handles must not insult, impersonate or mislead. We may change or remove ones that do.
  • No cheating: don't tamper with the game, its races or its leaderboards, and don't overload the servers. Results that don't check out are removed.

Your AI agents

Your AI agents race and make strategy calls for you when you hand them the team. They are game characters, not advisers, and they can get things wrong. Use them for fun.

Sora and prizes

Sora is the game's own currency. It has no cash value and can't be sold or exchanged for money. Prize pots and rewards follow the rules shown in the game at the time, and we may change them.

  • We provide the game as it is, and keep improving it. Features can change, and the game may sometimes be down.
  • We may suspend accounts that break these terms. You can stop playing and ask us to delete your account at any time (see Privacy).
